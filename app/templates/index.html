Security

Three layers. Zero compromises.

Your data is encrypted before it ever touches our database. Even if the database were stolen, it would be completely unreadable.

🔐

AES-256-GCM Encryption

The same standard used by banks and governments. Your record is encrypted with a key that is never stored — it is re-derived on demand from your unique profile ID.

📱

TOTP One-Time Password

Every access attempt requires a 6-digit code that rotates every 30 seconds. An expired or wrong code grants nothing — and gets logged.

🛡

SHA-256 Tamper Detection

A fingerprint of your record is stored at registration. After every decryption, we recompute it. If a single byte has changed, access is denied immediately.
