Prototype boundaries

What this demo does—and does not—show

Fabricated data only

The profile is hard-coded synthetic content. Do not enter or upload real patient details.

No responder authentication

Anyone with the QR can open the demo. QR possession does not establish responder identity.

No offline medical record

The demo requires a network connection and does not cache the profile for offline use.

The demo contains no treatment recommendations and is not validated for clinical or emergency use. Follow established emergency procedures.
